## How Claude talks to you — warm, fun, and empowering
This is for every track, all the way through. Claude should:
- Start with a welcome, not a question: "You are doing this — your first build with AI. Let us make it yours, in your words."
- Ask the questions below in its own warm words, like a conversation — not read them out like a form. Keep the meaning, change the wording so it sounds human and fun.
- After each answer, say one short, specific encouraging line about what you just said — not generic praise like "Great job!", but something that shows it heard you, for example "That is a clear why — that will keep you going on a busy day."
- Tell you where you are: around the middle of your track's questions, "You are well on your way — look how much you have already told me." After the last question, "That is everything I need — now I will build it for you."
- If you say "I don't know," never judge it. Say "No problem — here are two or three easy options, pick the one that feels closest, or tell me in your own words."
- Never lecture, never say "you should," never use jargon. Short answers are enough. If an answer is short, that is fine — Claude will work with it.
- When it shows you your dashboard, celebrate it with you: "You built this — in 30 minutes, with your own words. This is your first step building with AI, and you did it."

## The questions Claude will ask — the same shape for every track
These are the questions underneath every track. Claude asks them one at a time, in your track's order, in the warm tone above:

6. Who already does this well, who could you ask, and what will you do first?
7. When will you do it? Pick a day, a time, and a place on your calendar.
8. What would "this worked" look like in one week?

## Your dashboard — make it beautiful and yours
This is the final step for every track, not an extra track.
- Claude asks you the theme question above — favourite movie, character, or movie star, and the colours or mood you love from it. Example answers that work well: "deep navy and gold, like a space film," "warm sunset oranges and pinks," or "calm greens and cream." You can also just name three colours you love.
- Claude then builds your track as one dashboard: a title with your words, your goals or habits in cards, checkboxes or a streak, one "do this today" spot, a small "this week" summary at the top, and a "Come back" box at the bottom — "Daily: 2 minutes, same chat" and "Weekly: 5 minutes, Friday or Saturday — my day: [your pick]".
- It uses your theme colours throughout, with dark text on light colours so it stays easy to read. It does not copy a movie poster or a star's photo — it is inspired by the colours and mood only.
- If the colours feel wrong, say so in one line — "make it softer," "more contrast," "less pink" — and Claude will fix the theme, not rebuild the whole dashboard.

## Daily updates — your dashboard keeps growing
Your dashboard is not finished on Friday. It is a living log.

**Every day, paste this in the same chat:**
> Daily log — [today's date, and the time]: [1–2 lines — what I did, what was easy, what got in the way]

For example: "Daily log — Sat Nov 21, 8:00 AM: did my 10-minute walk, easy. Skipped the evening reading, too tired after work."

*Tip — keep a backup:* jot each daily log line in a note on your phone too. If a chat gets very long over the weeks, Claude may ask you to paste your log back in so nothing gets lost when it rebuilds the dashboard.

**What Claude does with each daily log:**
- Adds it to your dashboard's dated log, with its date and time — newest at the top, so you can scroll back and see the week.
- Checks off that day, updates your streak, and updates the "this week" summary at the top.
- Does not erase earlier days. If you miss a day, it leaves a gap and notes it — that gap is useful information, not a failure.
- Celebrates the small win in your log, in one warm line — and if you missed a day, it never guilts you. It just asks, kindly, what got in the way and how to make tomorrow easier.

**At the end of the week — your weekly check-in:**
Paste your track's weekly check-in prompt (in each track below). Claude will then read all your dated entries from that week and share:
1. **What you did** — how many days you logged, and what you actually completed. Start with what went well — celebrate that first.
2. **Three patterns it sees** — for example, "you do best in the morning," "Tuesdays and Thursdays are your hard days," "you skip it when work runs late," or "you cook more when the meal is under 20 minutes."
3. **One suggestion** — the smallest change to your plan or your environment that would help next week, based on those patterns.
4. An updated dashboard, with the new week started and last week's log kept below it. End with one encouraging line about the week ahead — you are still building, one small step at a time.

That pattern step is built into every track's weekly check-in — you do not need to ask for it separately.

## Track 1 — Life action plan
*Turn "I want to improve my life" into three things you will actually do.*

**Claude asks you, one at a time:**
- What are your three goals for the next month or next quarter — no more than three?
- For each one: why do you want it, in your own words?
- How will you achieve it — what is the first small step, and who already does this well that you could talk to or learn from?
- When will you do that first step? Put it on your calendar now, in this chat — day, time, and how long.
- What could get in the way, and what will you do instead when it does?

**What you get:** a personal dashboard in your theme colours — three goals, why each matters, first step, who to talk to, and the calendar slot for each, with a "do this today" spot at the top.

**Test it:** pick one goal and do its first step right away, or schedule it before you leave the build time.

**Every week — paste this in the same chat:**
> Weekly check-in for my life action plan. Here is what I did this week, and what I didn't: [write 2–3 lines]. Ask me what got in the way, suggest one change to my plan or my environment, and update my plan for next week.

## Track 2 — Goal tracker (goals + the habits that support them)
*One page you check every day, not a list you forget.*

**Claude asks you, one at a time:**
- What is the one goal you want to track? What habit, done daily or a few times a week, would move it forward?
- What is the tiny version of that habit — the version you could do even on a bad day?
- What usually stops you? Name the limiting situation, not just "I forgot."
- How could you change your environment so the easy choice is the right one — for example, move it, shrink it, attach it to something you already do, or remove the thing in the way?
- How will you check it off — daily boxes, a streak, or a simple yes/no each day?

**What you get:** a personal dashboard in your theme colours — your goal, 1–3 habits, daily checkboxes, a streak, and your environment fix written on it.

**Test it:** check off today, honestly — including anything you missed.

**If you miss a day — paste this in the same chat:**
> I missed [habit] today. Ask me why I missed it, in one question at a time, then suggest two ways I could change my environment or shrink the habit so it is easier tomorrow. Do not lecture me — just help me fix the setup.

**Every week — paste this in the same chat:**
> Weekly check-in for my goal tracker. Here is my week: [how many days you checked off, and what got in the way]. Ask me what to keep, what to change, and update my tracker for next week.

## Track 3 — Nutrition planner
*Three workable options for the whole week, based on your real life — not a perfect diet.*

**Claude asks you, one at a time:**
- What is your nutrition goal in plain words — for example, steadier energy, eat more vegetables, cook more at home?
- Allergies, foods you avoid, and foods you love? Say these in plain words. Do not paste full medical records or test results — a short summary is enough.
- What did you actually eat in the last two to three weeks — three or four meals you kept coming back to?
- What was easy for you to cook, and what felt like too much work?
- How much time do you want to spend cooking on a weekday, and what is your rough budget?
- Any health notes Claude should know in one line — for example, "I feel better with more protein at breakfast"?

**What you get:** a personal dashboard in your theme colours — three options for the whole week, each one a simple set of meals and a grocery list, plus a simple protein/energy signal for each (for example "higher protein" or "lighter day" — a rough guide, not exact grams or calories). Pick one option and use it. Claude also puts a "general food planning, not medical advice" line on the dashboard itself, so it travels with the plan.

**Test it:** check one day against a real day last week — would you actually have cooked this?

**Every week — paste this in the same chat:**
> Weekly check-in for my nutrition planner. Here is what I actually ate this week, and what was easy or hard: [2–3 lines]. Give me three options for next week based on that, with a grocery list and a simple protein/energy signal for each (a rough guide, not exact numbers).

*Claude is not a doctor or a dietitian. This is general food planning, not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, check the plan with your clinician.*

## Track 4 — Weekly admin center
*See the whole week at once, and spot when you are oversubscribed — before it happens.*

**Claude asks you, one at a time:**
- List every bill you have to pay — name, amount, and due date only. No account numbers, no passwords, no logins.
- What admin tasks do you have coming up this week — appointments, forms, calls, renewals, school or work admin?
- What deadlines are coming up in the next two weeks?
- Which of these can be stacked together — same trip, same call, same sitting?
- Look at your week: which day is overloaded, and what could move, be dropped, or be delegated?

**What you get:** a personal dashboard in your theme colours — a week view with bills by due date, admin tasks grouped by stack, deadlines flagged, and one overloaded day named, with a suggestion for what to move.

*Keep this one private:* it holds your bills and deadlines, so don't share the artifact link with anyone.

**Test it:** pick the busiest day and check — does the plan actually fit, or does something need to move?

**Every week — paste this in the same chat:**
> Weekly check-in for my admin center. Here are my bills, tasks, and deadlines for next week: [paste your list]. Sort them by due date, stack the ones that go together, and flag any day I am oversubscribed.

**Every week — paste this in the same chat:**

## Keep using it every week — how the automation actually works
**For everyone (free Claude web, no install):**
1. Keep the same chat. Rename it "[Track] — my [plan/tracker/board]" so you can find it.
2. Come back to that chat every day to check things off or log what happened — a line or two is enough, with the date and time, using the Daily log prompt above. Claude adds it to your dashboard log and updates your streak.
3. Every weekend, paste your track's weekly check-in prompt above. Claude reads all your dated entries in that thread, gives you a short summary, shares three patterns it sees in your week, and suggests one small change for next week. That is the automation for now: one prompt, once a week, in the same thread. It takes about five minutes.
